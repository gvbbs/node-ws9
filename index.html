About Our Mission

OceanGuard is a non-profit organization dedicated to the preservation and restoration of our world's oceans. Founded in 2010, we work tirelessly to address the critical threats facing marine ecosystems.

Our initiatives focus on reducing plastic pollution, protecting endangered marine species, restoring coral reefs, and promoting sustainable fishing practices. Through research, education, and direct action, we strive to create a healthier ocean for all.

We believe that every individual can contribute to ocean conservation, and we provide tools, resources, and opportunities for people around the world to get involved in protecting our blue planet.

Critical Ocean Issues

Plastic Pollution

Over 8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans each year, harming marine life and ecosystems. Microplastics are now found in even the most remote marine environments.

Coral Reef Bleaching

Rising ocean temperatures cause coral bleaching, destroying vital reef ecosystems that support 25% of all marine species and protect coastlines from storms.

Overfishing

Nearly 90% of the world's fish stocks are fully exploited or overfished, threatening food security for billions and destabilizing marine ecosystems.
